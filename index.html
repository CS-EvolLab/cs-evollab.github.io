Shiji Zhou 周仕佶

Associate Professor

I am a associate professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence and the Advanced Innovation Center for Future Blockchain and Privacy Computing at Beihang University. My research focuses on machine learning and optimization for intelligent agents, including embodied agents.

I received a B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from the Chinese Academy of Sciences–Beihang University Hua Luogeng Mathematics Honors Class and a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University under the supervision of Prof. Wenwu Zhu. From 2023 to 2025, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, working with Academician Qionghai Dai.

I have published more than 30 papers at international conferences and in journals, including NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR. I also serve as an area chair for all three conferences.

CS-EVOL LAB

Research

The Controllable Self-Evolving Intelligence Lab studies how agents can continue learning in open environments while respecting behavioral and performance constraints.

Constitutional AI

Encode rules and preferences that guide behavior and constrain how agents improve.

Open-ended learning

Generate tasks and experience, develop agent memory, and improve policies and harnesses across iterations.

Multi-objective optimization

Use ordered preferences and constraints to guide decisions, reinforcement learning post-training, and embodied agent adaptation.
